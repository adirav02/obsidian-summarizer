# Conditional Expectation

For discrete random variables, the conditional expectation of X given Y=y is

$$
E[X \mid Y=y] = \sum_x x P(X=x \mid Y=y).
$$

The law of total expectation states

$$
E[X] = E[E[X \mid Y]].
$$

Example: choose one of two boxes with equal probability. Box A always pays 10, while Box B pays 0 or 20 with equal probability. Conditioning on the selected box reveals how the overall expectation is assembled from local expectations.
